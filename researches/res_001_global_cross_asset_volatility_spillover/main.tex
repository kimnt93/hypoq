\begin{abstract}
\noindent \textbf{Hypothesis:} $H_0:\beta_{\text{US}\to\text{Asia/Europe}}=0$ versus $H_1:\beta>0$ for the next-session response of the Nikkei 225 and Euro Stoxx 50 to the prior S\&P 500 return \\
\textbf{Data:} Daily index returns, 2016-10-25 to 2026-10-23, 2,304 sessions common to all three markets \\
\textbf{Method:} OLS with Newey--West HAC errors (5 lags), a 2000-draw circular-shift placebo, and a Cholesky-FEVD spillover index from a BIC-selected VAR(1) \\
\textbf{Results:} Nikkei: $\beta=0.500$, $t=7.70$, placebo $p=0.0005$; Euro Stoxx: $\beta=0.118$, $t=1.93$, placebo $p=0.0290$. Decision threshold: Bonferroni $\alpha=0.025$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Hypothesis and Design}
Asian and European sessions close before the US session, so the previous US return is strictly predetermined \citep{engle1990meteor}. The test is a one-sided HAC regression with a circular-shift placebo that preserves serial dependence while breaking the cross-market alignment.

\section{Results}
The FEVD total spillover index is 22.4\% with ordering US, EU, JP and 24.5\% with the reverse ordering; Cholesky FEVD is order dependent, so the gap between the two is reported as sensitivity \citep{diebold2012better}.

\section{Limitations}
Only sessions present in all three calendars are used; no transaction costs or trading rule are evaluated; returns rather than realised volatility are modelled.
