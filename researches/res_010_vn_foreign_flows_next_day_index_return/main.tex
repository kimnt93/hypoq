\begin{abstract}
\noindent \textbf{Hypothesis:} $H_0$: foreign flow imbalance on day $t$ has no relation to the VN-Index return on $t+1$; $H_1$: the relation is positive \\
\textbf{Data:} 2,027 sessions, 2018-08-30 to 2026-10-22; imbalance aggregated over up to 65 stocks \\
\textbf{Method:} OLS with Newey--West HAC errors (5 lags) and a 2,000-draw circular-shift placebo; ACCEPTED only if both one-sided $p<0.05$ \\
\textbf{Results:} $\beta=0.0010$ (HAC $t=0.64$, $p=0.2610$; placebo $p=0.3068$), $R^2=0.0002$; controlling for the same-day return $t=0.20$. \\
\textbf{Research decision:} \colorbox[rgb]{0.75,0.15,0.15}{\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\,REJECTED\,}}}
\end{abstract}

\section{Design}
Vietnamese exchanges publish foreign buy and sell values at the end of each session. The imbalance is therefore known at the close of day $t$ and is paired with the return of day $t+1$ only. Sessions with zero reported foreign turnover are vendor gaps and are dropped, never filled. Foreign ownership limits make flows informative or merely price-following, see \citet{griffin2004foreign}.

\section{Results}
The contemporaneous correlation between the imbalance and the same-day return is 0.134, so flows largely follow returns. With the same-day return as a control the coefficient on the imbalance is 0.0003 ($t=0.20$).

\section{Limitations}
The 65 stocks are those currently tracked, so delisted names are missing. Flows are aggregated in VND without adjustment for market capitalisation.
