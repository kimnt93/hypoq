\begin{abstract}
\noindent \textbf{Hypothesis:} $H_0$: the VN30 futures basis at day $t$ has no relation to the VN30 return on $t+1$; $H_1$: the relation is positive \\
\textbf{Data:} 2,285 sessions, 2017-08-24 to 2026-10-22 (0 sessions with $|$basis$|>10\%$ dropped) \\
\textbf{Method:} OLS with Newey--West HAC errors (5 lags) and a 2,000-draw circular-shift placebo; ACCEPTED only if both one-sided $p<0.05$ \\
\textbf{Results:} $\beta=0.0319$ (HAC $t=0.55$, $p=0.2921$; placebo $p=0.2779$), $R^2=0.0003$; top-minus-bottom basis quintile next-day return 0.168\%. \\
\textbf{Research decision:} \colorbox[rgb]{0.75,0.15,0.15}{\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\,REJECTED\,}}}
\end{abstract}

\section{Design}
The VN30 futures are the only liquid index derivative in Vietnam and retail-heavy, so the basis may capture sentiment before it reaches the cash market \citep{hasbrouck2003intraday}. Both prices are daily closes of the same session; the return of the next session is the outcome, hence there is no overlap with the predictor.

\section{Limitations}
The series is a continuous front-month series, so the basis is mechanically linked to time to expiry and to dividends, which are not modelled. Daily closes cannot reveal intraday lead--lag effects.
